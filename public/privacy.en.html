Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short: we don't collect any of your information, and we couldn't if we wanted to.

We have no servers

The Zyll Photos web version is a purely static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It runs in your browser and connects directly to your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Your photos, file names, account details and passwords never pass through any Zyll server, because we don't have one.

Where your data lives

  • Photos and the backup index are stored in your own cloud storage, under your control.
  • Sign-in tokens and a few settings (such as the auto-lock time) are kept only in your current browser's local storage. Disconnecting an account or clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Encrypted photos are decrypted only in your browser. Your password is never stored or sent anywhere.

Third parties

When you sign in and read or write your cloud storage, requests go directly to Google or Microsoft. When you open the website, GitHub handles the page requests. These companies process those requests under their own privacy policies; Zyll cannot see that data.

The website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
